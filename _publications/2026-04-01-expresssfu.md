A WebRTC SFU that offloads the media data plane — SRTP crypto, packet forwarding, and simulcast/SVC layer selection — onto the Arm cores of an NVIDIA BlueField-2 SmartNIC, cutting media-packet latency 8× and serving 3× more concurrent users than the software baseline.
