Real-time deepfake detection as a native SFU service: detects deepfake onsets directly on the encoded VP9 bitstream and offloads crypto and NIC-to-GPU frame movement to a BlueField-3 DPU — ~144× lower per-frame processing latency and 26× more concurrent conferences at 95.8% precision.
