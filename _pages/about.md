I am a CS Ph.D. student at the **University of Colorado Boulder**, advised by [Prof. Mark Zhao](https://home.cs.colorado.edu/~myzhao/) and [Prof. Sangtae Ha](https://netstech.org/sangtaeha/). I work on **ML systems** — currently **cluster-wide checkpointing for multi-tenant LLM training** — and systems for large-scale real-time video. My thesis builds a controller that sets each training job's checkpoint frequency, where each copy goes (host memory, local disk, peer nodes' memory, or a remote store), and how much network bandwidth it uses. I am validating it on an 8-GPU A100 cluster on Jetstream2.

I am a co-author of **eXpressSFU** (USENIX **NSDI '26**) and **DeepSFU** (ACM **SIGCOMM '26**), which offload the media data plane of video-conferencing infrastructure onto SmartNICs/DPUs.

Before my Ph.D., I was a UK **Chevening Scholar**, and earlier worked as a DevOps engineer at **Skyroom**, Iran's leading video-conferencing platform, operating production infrastructure through its ~100× COVID-19 scale-up.
